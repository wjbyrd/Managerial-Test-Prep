How This Works

Choose an operation to begin reviewing.

Each operation focuses on a different part of the course and adapts to your performance as you play.

  • Correct answers move you forward.
  • Missed questions may send you back or trigger review.
  • Boss rooms check whether you are ready to advance.
  • Completion is reported when you finish an operation
  • Progress is saved at your current room. You can close the browser and pick up where you last were.

When you enter an operation, use your school email on that game’s start screen so your progress can be recorded.
